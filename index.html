</> Friday Session Quest Format (11:00 – 14:00)

Every Friday session in Room 515 is structured like an interactive engineering quest:

11:00 10 min
Spawn Point
Welcome everyone, announce the weekly quest, show leaderboard updates, and let members choose their difficulty.
Reward: Participation XP (+10 XP)
11:10 45 min
Main Quest
Work through a LeetCode-style problem, debugging challenge, or small team exercise. Starter route & optional harder route.
Reward: Challenge XP (+10 to +25 XP) & skill badges
11:55 10 min
Save Point
Break, social time, coffee, and optional bonus brainteaser puzzle.
12:05 45 min
Skill Tree
Workshop, mock interviews, guest speaker, or project work. Members unlock skill-specific achievements.
Reward: Workshop XP (+15 XP)
12:50 50 min
Party Quest
Pair programming, peer review, mock interviews, or a collaborative mini-project. Rotate teams to meet everyone.
Reward: Peer Help (+10 XP) & Project Contribution (+25 XP)
13:40 20 min
Victory Screen
Share solutions, award badges, celebrate progress, reveal the next quest, and collect quick feedback.
Reward: Solution Explanation (+10 XP)

💡 Our Culture & Principles

The kind of club we build depends on how we treat one another:

Curiosity over ego

Ask questions freely. Stay open to being wrong and learning in public.

Progress over perfection

Small steps count. Consistent practice beats waiting for perfect code.

Collaboration over competition

Share credit, review each other’s code, and help teammates succeed.

Respect at every level

Make room for different backgrounds, study years, and experience levels.

🛡️ Three Rules to Prevent XP Farming

  • Verified Achievements: Points are awarded for verified contributions and solutions, not simply claiming them.
  • Depth & Teamwork: We reward learning, clear explanations, and peer help—not just the fastest solver.
  • Balanced Caps: Repeatable actions (like peer-help XP) have a reasonable session cap so activity stays authentic.
